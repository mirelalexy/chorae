# Chorae

You know the feeling. You've made five playlists for cold winter nights, and somehow none of them quite get it right. You've had the same song on repeat for three days, but you still don't know where it belongs. Maybe you have a feeling you can't quite put into words, or a song that captures it perfectly but nothing else that sounds the same. Maybe you just want to discover something new, something that feels like a part of you that you haven't quite met yet.

That's what Chorae is for.

Chorae is a place to make sense of your musical taste, explore what draws you to certain songs, and turn the feelings you can't quite describe into something you can listen to.

At the center of it all is Rae, your personal mixtape maker. Tell her what you're looking for, and she'll help you make it come true. Every track has a reason for being there, and every mixtape has a story of its own.

Not just a playlist. A mixtape.

---

## Features

### Describe a feeling, get a mixtape

You don't need to know the genre, the artists, or even exactly what you're looking for. Just tell Rae what's on your mind.

**A walk home.**

> *It's cold outside and I'm walking home from uni, nobody's waiting for me tonight. It's just me. I want something that feels like running through the city, letting myself be free. Feel it all.*

**A character you're writing.**

> *I'm trying to understand the main character of my book more. He's a mercenary who's starting to remember why he stopped letting himself feel things. He's arrogant, charming, but also hopeless... completely exhausted. He is slowly starting to care about a woman he once hated. I need to feel that battle inside his head.*

**A chore you can't get yourself to start.**

> *I always feel like a zombie when I know I need to start cleaning up my room... I need something that makes starting feel less impossible.*

**A slow winter morning.**

> *I want something to play during cold winter mornings. Right when I wake up and make my hot chocolate. Something warm and delicate that makes me feel seen, like being wrapped up in a blanket.*

**A world you can't stop thinking about.**

> *I want to feel like I'm walking through the RPD or an abandoned lab. Something that brings me closer to what Leon Kennedy is like in Resident Evil Requiem. Dark, dramatic, but still cool. He's been my favorite character for years.*

Your prompt can be as vague, detailed, or oddly specific as you want. That's part of the fun.

Before building anything, Rae asks a few questions to get a better sense of what you mean: songs that already capture the feeling, how you want the mixtape to end, how intense it should get, whether you want familiar artists or new discoveries, how long it should be, and whether it should tell a story.

> **Rae:** *What should it sound like? Any songs that already feel like this?*
>
> **You:** *"Psycho" by Muse, "Impose" by Bad Omens, "I Can Fix You" by Jutes.*

From there, Rae puts together a mixtape using real, playable tracks. If something doesn't feel right, you can tell her what you'd like to change and try again. Each attempt becomes a new version, so you can always go back to an earlier one.

### Remake a playlist you already have

Sometimes you know what you want a playlist to be, but the songs never quite come together.

Import an existing playlist as a file or a pasted list, and Rae will help you reshape it. She matches the tracks, keeps what works, rearranges the rest into sides, and builds a more cohesive arc. She'll also explain what she changed and why.

Your original playlist stays untouched. You're free to experiment without losing what you started with.

### Sides, notes, and stories

A mixtape isn't just a collection of songs in a particular order. It's about how they work together.

Chorae divides each mixtape into sides, each with its own mood and place in the overall arc. A Resident Evil-inspired mixtape might move through *the station after hours*, *something in the vents*, and *requiem*, while a late-night city walk might move from *out past midnight* to *nothing but silence*, before *still alone*. A breakup mixtape could start with *when it was good*, work its way through *all the things you left behind* and *wherever the wind takes me next*. Every track gets a short note explaining why it's there and what it adds to the mixtape.

You can also ask Rae to tell a story across the whole thing.

A slow winter morning might unfold into a snow day, moving from the still-dark early hours to the afternoon light. A character falling in love against their will might go from absolutely not to getting harder to ignore, until there's no point pretending anymore. And a mixtape made for someone you miss might read like a letter you never quite knew how to write.

The songs are still the focus. The story just gives them something to hold on to.

### Your taste, on your terms

Your most played are not always your most loved. Instead of guessing from your listening history, Chorae lets you say it: the artists, songs and albums that are actually yours. Rae uses them to find what scratches the same itch, and to keep *fresh finds* genuinely fresh. Your taste profile is visible only to you, and you can delete it whenever you want to start over.

### Real songs, honest recommendations

Every track is checked against [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org) before it's saved. Nothing goes on a mixtape unless it's a real, playable song. If one of the songs you asked for can't be found, Rae tells you instead of slipping in a lookalike.

### Listen wherever you like

Chorae doesn't stream music. Your mixtapes live in Chorae, and you can take them to whichever music app you prefer.

- **Open in...**: Open individual tracks in your music app.
- **Export**: Export a mixtape as a file and import it into Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Tidal, Deezer, and other services using a transfer tool.
- **Share**: Send someone a link to your mixtape that they can open without an account.

Direct saving to YouTube Music is planned. For more on how streaming integrations work, see [Why Chorae doesn't connect to Spotify](#why-chorae-doesnt-connect-to-spotify).

## Why Chorae doesn't connect to Spotify

Chorae originally started as a Spotify companion. The idea was simple: sign in, let Rae learn from your listening history, and have her create playlists directly in your account.

That version cannot exist, since Spotify's developer terms don't allow apps to analyze Spotify content or use it with AI models, and access to its API for new apps is now limited to services that already have a large user base.

Chorae was redesigned to stand on its own:

- **Your taste comes from you.** Rae works with the music you choose to share, not from your listening history.
- **Songs are identified through [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org).** Track matching relies on an open music encyclopedia rather than a streaming service's catalog.
- **Your mixtapes belong to you.** They live in Chorae and aren't tied to a single streaming platform.

This approach also makes Chorae more flexible. You can switch music services without having to start your mixtapes from scratch.

### Getting a mixtape into your music app

**Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, Deezer, and other services**

Export your mixtape as a file, then use a playlist transfer tool such as [TuneMyMusic](https://www.tunemymusic.com) or [Soundiiz](https://soundiiz.com) to import it into your library.

**Just looking for a song?**

Use the **Open in...** link on any track to find it in your preferred music app.

**YouTube Music**

Direct saving from Chorae is planned.

**Want Rae to remake an existing playlist?**

Export the playlist from your music service using a transfer tool, then bring it into Chorae as a file or a plain list of tracks.

## Meet Rae

Rae makes mixtapes the way some people make playlists for their friends: by paying attention to the little details.

She wants to know what you're looking for, but she also knows that you might not have the words for it yet. Maybe you have three songs that almost capture the same feeling. Maybe you know exactly how you want a mixtape to end but have no idea how to get there. Maybe you just want to hear something you've never heard before.

That's where Rae comes in.

She's the creative voice behind Chorae, helping you turn a vague idea into a mixtape that feels considered, personal, and worth coming back to. She asks questions when she needs to, makes suggestions when she has something useful to offer, and leaves room for you to change your mind.

### Music comes first

Rae cares about more than finding songs that fit a description. She thinks about how they sound together, what happens when one ends and another begins, and how the feeling of a mixtape changes as you listen.

A song can fit the mood perfectly and still be in the wrong place. Sometimes the track you almost left out turns out to be the one that holds everything together.

That's the kind of thing she's trying to get right.

And instead of calling something *atmospheric indie with post-rock influences*, Rae tries to explain what makes it work: a bassline that sounds like footsteps on tile, a chorus that opens like a car window, or drums that arrive just when you need them.

### You decide where to head next

You don't have to get your prompt perfect on the first try. Rae can help you figure out what's missing, but the final call is always yours.

If a recommendation doesn't fit, tell her why. If you want less of something, more of something else, or an entirely different direction, you can build on the previous version without losing it. And if your original playlist already works, there's no reason to change it just for the sake of changing it.

### She has a voice of her own

Rae is thoughtful without being overly serious, curious without asking questions just to fill the silence, and occasionally a little sarcastic. She will sometimes check on you or give you mixtape ideas you might like exploring.

Sometimes a short explanation is enough. Sometimes the best thing she can do is admit that she missed the mark and try again. She's not here to tell you what your music means or what you should be feeling. She's here to help you explore it.

### The more you share, the more personal it gets

Rae only works with what you tell her and what you create in Chorae. She doesn't have access to your listening history, and she doesn't pretend to know things you haven't shared.

Over time, she can use the context available within Chorae to notice patterns: the moods you return to, the artists you keep coming back to, the mixtape you've remade three times, or realizing that your idea of a fun playlist also includes adding sad songs... you might wonder why you do that.

The point isn't to put you in a box or build a profile that claims to explain who you are. It's to give Rae better context for making something you'll actually want to listen to.

### What you can expect from her

- **Honest recommendations.** She won't make up a track she cannot verify.
- **Thoughtful explanations.** Her notes explain why a song belongs, without overanalyzing every lyric or spelling out every emotion.
- **Room to experiment.** Your feedback shapes the next version, and your earlier versions stay available.
- **No assumptions about you.** She can ask what a song means to you, but she won't pretend to know the answer.
- **A clear sense of what she is.** Rae is an AI music companion, not a person pretending to have a life, memories, or musical experiences outside Chorae.

## How it works

1. **Describe what you're looking for.** Start with a feeling, a situation, a few reference songs, or an existing playlist you want to remake.
2. **Rae asks a few questions.** She asks two to four questions based on what's missing from your prompt.
3. **The mixtape takes shape.** Rae drafts the sides, track order, notes, optional story, and backup picks. Her response is structured and validated before the mixtape is saved.
4. **Tracks are verified.** Chorae matches recordings through [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org), using available metadata such as recording IDs, duration, and ISRCs. Cover art is retrieved from the [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org/). If a recommendation can't be matched, a verified backup can take its place. If one of your requested songs can't be found, Rae tells you.
5. **Every attempt becomes a version.** Your first mixtape is version 1. Feedback creates version 2, version 3, and so on. Earlier versions remain available instead of being overwritten.

## Tech stack

- **Mobile and web:** Expo, React Native, Expo Router, TypeScript.
- **API:** Node, Express, PostgreSQL, TypeScript.
- **AI:** Claude API, with structured output validated using Zod.
- **Music metadata:** MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive.

## Project structure

```text
chorae/
├── api/                    Node + Express + PostgreSQL
│   ├── migrations/         SQL files, run in order by the migration script
│   └── src/
│       ├── config.ts       Environment settings and startup validation
│       ├── app.ts          Express middleware, routes, and error handling
│       ├── server.ts       Server startup

│       ├── routes/         HTTP endpoints
│       ├── musicbrainz/    Search, track matching, and cover art
│       ├── rae/            Rae's prompts and response schemas
│       └── jobs/           Worker that builds mixtapes step by step
└── mobile/                 Expo app
    ├── app/                Screens and routes (Expo Router)
    └── src/                Components, theme, and API client
```

## Status

**Version 1 is in progress.**

The current scope includes accounts, taste profiles, creating and remaking mixtapes, version history, export, and sharing.

### What's next after that?

- **Playlist cleanup.** Bring in the five versions of "rainy days when i tend to overthink" you've been meaning to fix, explain what they were supposed to feel like, and let Rae help combine them into one cohesive mixtape.
- **Update from your copy.** Bring changes you've made in your streaming app back into Chorae as a new version, with a clear comparison of what changed.
- **YouTube Music integration.** Save mixtapes directly to your library.
- **Custom cover art.** Upload your own image for a mixtape.
- **Reviews and communities.** Explore albums, artists, and mixtapes and see what others have to say.

### Ideas for the future

**Your listening journal**

A place to leave thoughts after listening, save favorite lyrics, and return to the music that meant something to you at a particular point in your life. Not another listening recap, but a record of what the music meant to you. Ask things like:

> *Why do I keep returning to heartbreak albums when I'm trying to move on? I keep listening to Olivia Rodrigo's "you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love" and it makes me feel worse...*

**Writing with Rae**

Share something you've written about a person, a memory, or an experience, and ask Rae what artists, writers, or songs it brings to mind. Not to imitate someone else's voice, but to explore your own and discover music that resonates with it.
